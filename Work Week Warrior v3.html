The Program

Built for 2×10 kg dumbbells + 1×20 kg.

Equipment-capped progression: with fixed dumbbells you progress by reps → tempo → pauses → less rest, not weight. When you hit the top of a rep range on all sets: add a 3-second negative. Master that? Add a 1-second pause at the hardest point. Then cut rest by 15s. Each step makes 10 kg feel heavier.

🏋️ 2×10 = both 10 kg dumbbells · 🏋️ 1×20 = the single 20 kg
